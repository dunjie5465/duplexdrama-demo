DuplexDrama: A Script-Aware Full-Duplex Spoken Dialogue Dataset with Sound Events

6,400 multi-turn spoken dialogues (800 h: Chinese ~500 h + English ~300 h) for full-duplex speech-language research

Paper Coming Soon Dataset Coming Soon Listen

Abstract

We present DuplexDrama, the first synthesized spoken dialogue dataset that combines four dimensions not simultaneously available in any prior corpus:

  1. Persona & Scenario — complete persona and scenario settings produced via LLMs;
  2. Full-duplex behaviors — three behaviors carried as tags: interruption, backchannel, and in-sentence pause, rendered as acoustic overlap rather than clean turn-taking;
  3. Expressive speech — wide speaker coverage with a closed seven-class emotion vocabulary aligned to persona;
  4. Script-aware sound events — sound events injected at proper positions parsed from the script, affording non-speech information beyond voice.

The release covers 6,400 dialogues totalling 800 hours (Chinese ∼500 h + English ∼300 h), built with a 64-voice timbre pool spanning 13 personas and 5 age buckets. Speech is synthesized with IndexTTS2; 3.8% of utterances carry at least one full-duplex status. An asset bank supplies 139 environment audios (across 7 scene categories) and 2,203 sound event audios (52 categories), both injected into the dialogue track through a match strategy. DeepSeek-v4.1-pro and Gemini-3.1-pro-preview serve as dual LLM judges for cross-checking both script content and dialogue audio. The full dataset will be released to advance full-duplex spoken dialogue model development; audio samples and the LLM-judge evaluation prompt live on this page.

Pipeline

Listen to DuplexDrama

6 excerpts of roughly 60–100 seconds, each cut from a full dialogue. Press play; the two waveforms are the HM and AI channels, so overlapping speech is visible directly. Both tracks carry their ambient background — click either channel label to mute it and hear them apart.

Watermarked: every excerpt is synthetic speech carrying an inaudible per-channel AudioSeal watermark (EU AI Act Art. 50), traceable via its .uuid.json sidecar.

Note: these are excerpts, not complete dialogues — cut this way so the dataset's characteristics are audible at a glance rather than buried in a several-minute recording. Expand "Transcript" to read every turn in the excerpt; the event turn is highlighted in amber.
